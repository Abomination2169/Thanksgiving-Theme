## Audio (public domain source)

The files `Thanksgiving-Gobble-High-Note-Fade.ogg` and `Thanksgiving-Gobble-Quiet-Phrase.ogg` were edited from the public-domain recording **Gobbler.ogg**, credited to **bod**, originally PDSounds.org recording #500 and archived at [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gobbler.ogg). The recording was cut and processed into two clips. The public-domain status of the source recording is separate from the source code's GPLv3 license.
