## Audio

The two turkey-gobble OGG clips are edited versions of the public-domain **Gobbler.ogg** recording attributed to **bod**, originally from PDSounds.org recording #500 and archived at [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gobbler.ogg). Files: `Thanksgiving-Gobble-High-Note-Fade.ogg` and `Thanksgiving-Gobble-Quiet-Phrase.ogg`. The public-domain source was cut/processed into two mod audio clips. Public-domain status is independent of the code's GPLv3 license.
